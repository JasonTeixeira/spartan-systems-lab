Self-contained interview prompts, role rubrics, and market vacancy benchmarks consolidated into `spartan-systems-lab` for Friday Track E reps and M12 Principal Capstone mock loops.

- **`seed-questions.json`** — 30 leveled architecture & scenario prompts across 6 competency families (`junior` → `staff/principal`) with expected elements, follow-ups, and visual whiteboard tasks.
- **`role-profiles.json`** — Target role competency profiles and leveling expectations across SWE, Backend, Cloud/SRE, Data, AI/LLM, and Security roles.
- **`vacancy-benchmark.json`** — Market role skill frequency & interview coverage benchmark.

Pair with **ArchOS** (`http://127.0.0.1:5180/`) for timed 45-minute visual whiteboards and **`INDEX 0.app`** (`/Applications/INDEX 0.app`) for offline cold DSA + debugging scenarios.
